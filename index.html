UNIT-7 Escape Protocol

An AI agent has to collect three keycards, crack a lock at the terminal, and reach the exit, while a drone patrols, ceilings collapse onto its route and the lock's rules change. It uses A* search to plan routes, constraint satisfaction to solve the lock, and a behaviour tree to decide what to do next. The drone is a small finite state machine. The simulation is written in Python and runs here in your browser through Pyodide.
